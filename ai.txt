# Independence Day - PropFirmDiscount.com

Independence Day prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

11 deals from 11 prop firms, newest 2026-07-04.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
